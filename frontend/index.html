About RESPARSE

What is RESPARSE?

RESPARSE is an AI-powered research paper retrieval system that helps researchers find high-quality, relevant scientific papers using semantic understanding and journal quality metrics.

Key Features

  • Semantic Search: Understands context and meaning, not just keywords
  • Quality Filtering: Only shows papers from high-impact journals (Q1/Q2)
  • Multi-Criteria Ranking: Balances semantic relevance, journal quality, and recency
  • AI Explanations: Explains why each paper is relevant to your query
